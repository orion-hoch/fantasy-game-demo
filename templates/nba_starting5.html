Draft your best NBA team, one random franchise at a time

How to Play
  • Each turn a random NBA franchise is drawn — name a player from that team
  • Select the season year to lock in your pick
  • Fill your roster: 2G · 2F · 1C · 1 UTIL (any position)
  • Each player can only be drafted once across all rosters
  • Old heads bonus: 1.5× score for a random position from the 70s–90s
  • Highest total fantasy score wins
  • Scoring: PTS +1 · 3PM +1 · FGM +2 · FGA −1 · FTM +1 · FTA −1 · REB +1 · AST +2 · STL +4 · BLK +4 · TOV −2
