Draw 5 cards and combine them two at a time — pick a card, an operation, then a second card. Keep going until one card remains that matches the target!

How To Play

  • 5 cards are dealt from a shuffled deck each round
  • A target number (24–99) is generated — guaranteed solvable
  • Click a card, then an operation, then a second card to combine them
  • Keep combining until 1 card remains — it must equal the target
  • Use Undo to take back a combine, or Reset to start over
  • You get 3 attempts per round across 5 rounds
  • Card values: A=1, 2–10, J=11, Q=12, K=13
