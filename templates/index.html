Fantasy Sports Trivia by Orion

Welcome

A collection of fantasy sports trivia games built around NFL and NBA players. Test your knowledge across different game formats — from chain puzzles and ticking time bombs to roguelike dungeon runs and Balatro-style card drafts.

Updates
March 2026
NBA Balatro Added

Draft hands of NBA player-season cards, build scoring combos, and beat the shot clock across multiple rounds. Features Guards, Forwards, and Centers with unique joker bonuses.

February 2026
Dungeon Adventure Expanded

Added NBA mode to Dungeon Adventure. Pick a class, collect relics that filter your player pool, and work through 6 dungeon floors of trivia combat.
